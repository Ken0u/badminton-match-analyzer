# Local revamp stroke experiments: fairness record

Runs are serialized through the real shared Store; none installs weights. The frozen local protocols, clip hashes, per-match artifacts and outputs live in `.cache/stroke-rgb-v1/` and `.cache/doubles-stroke-window-v1/`.

* **Singles:** five native-time grayscale hitter crops from each predicted contact. On missing or unverified footage or any missing crop, fall back to the previous motion prediction; never omit that contact to score the visual model. Compare the same hits and matched labels against both frozen revamp baselines with historical 2/4-frame scoring. Repeat validation is development only. Do not claim RGB gains on AICup if only its pose/shuttle features are available.
* **Doubles:** train a four-player motion classifier on 2,831 DShuttleNet feature files from 30 published training matches. Validation inputs: 508 feature files from six DShuttleNet matches and 69 from S2 Doubles match 2, a separate benchmark. DShuttleNet copies of S2 validation/test matches are excluded; S2 match 1 is not also trained on top of its DShuttleNet copy. Every arm uses the same v5 contacts and predicted hitter/end/partner. The v4 family head on those same contacts is a secondary comparator, not a reproduction of native v4 contact accuracy. Report unknown hitter support separately. Coarse source labels cannot prove independent push vs net-kill or net vs block recognition. Research data and weights remain subject to existing terms.
* **Decision:** freeze weights, blends, timing, source splits and class definitions before scoring. Count newly introduced as well as corrected errors, and report per-source variation and the denominator, including exclusions upstream of the historical scorer. A small positive pooled change is not enough. For doubles require at least +2 points six-family macro F1 and +3 points on drive/lift/drop, improvement on at least four of six DShuttleNet validation matches, no decline in complete-event F1 or S2 six-family macro F1. Do not equate correctness of a visible tracked slot at a contact with persistent partner identity. A fresh, independent whole-match evaluation is needed before claiming either mode meets the product goal. No sealed test split is opened by these runs.

### Original-match audit (local source tables, November 18)

Cross-checked the actual frozen doubles file inventory against DShuttleNet `match.csv` / `player.csv` and S2 `Match.csv`. Key: calendar date plus the unordered four full player names (case-insensitive), independent of corpus match IDs. The 30/6 DShuttleNet train/validation matches have no shared key or tournament ID. S2 matches 1, 2 and 3 resolve to DShuttleNet 5, 6 and 7 respectively. Only 5 appears in training; neither 6 nor 7 appears in either DShuttleNet arm. S2 validation is **one** distinct match, not 69 independent observations. This checks supplied source metadata, not upstream model pretraining.

For singles, ShuttleSet validation includes two different Marin vs. An matches (35 and 38, January 23 and 29, 2021): even the two player names alone would be an invalid deduplication key. The AI CUP Bangkok training group's 132 clip memberships are venue-level, without dates and players in the checked capture-group manifest. Overlap with the five ShuttleSet validation matches is **unresolved**, not established or ruled out. Keep the original denominator for the paired development comparison; do not describe it as a certified source-disjoint benchmark.

### Doubles result: consistent development gain, below the frozen threshold

Fixed two-frame comparison: DShuttleNet v5 5,513/6,510 known matched strokes vs four-player window 5,650/6,510 (+137). Net correct rose on all six matches (+22, +25, +15, +20, +28, +27). Six-family macro F1 .87258 -> .88961 (+1.70 points); drive/lift/drop macro .85057 -> .86758 (+1.70); complete-event F1 .66535 -> .68233. Drive gained 2.80, lift 1.20 and drop 1.11 points. The predeclared +2 and +3 macro gates both fail. S2's one validation match went from 451/573 to 459/573, six-family .81367 -> .84669; weak macro .84432 -> .84497 (drop declined .76 points). Four-frame DShuttleNet net gain +143/6,644; S2 +6/663. No test inference, no promotion or post hoc blend selection. This is a family-head comparison on fixed detected contacts, not a new tracker or a persistent partner-identity result. 68 DShuttleNet reference rows were excluded upstream from both arms; 6,886 reference events before exclusions. Exact paired transitions are recovered separately against the frozen report, not inferred from the net differences.

### Singles result: grayscale specialist falls short

ShuttleSet, historical two-frame tolerance: the ensemble, window and visual heads scored .80068, .80275 and .80959 matched-family macro F1. Weak-family macro F1 was .73143, .73002 and .73487, respectively. With 2,036 matched known contacts, the visual head fixed 24 errors and introduced 17 vs the window (four of five matches positive); vs the ensemble it fixed 38 and introduced 27 (three of five positive). Both historical baselines reproduced exactly on both tolerances. No gain on AICup is established. The specialist misses both required macro gains. Retain as research only; no external acceptance scoring or validation-driven reblend. For the next candidate, learn from longer action intervals and original-match-grouped training diagnostics rather than independently repeating this short-crop experiment on the same validation sources. In particular FineBadminton 0044 is visibly a Lucknow Sen/Teh highlights edit, not a full match: quarantine from whole-match evaluation and do not treat inter-cut gaps as negative events.

### Longer flight-video model: training-group diagnostic

All 30 historical training-match packs completed. Fit the three-view head for 24 fixed epochs on 13,767 contacts from 27 matches. On the three Indonesia Masters matches withheld only from this head, at +/-2 frames the window baseline got 1,244/1,247 matched labels right and the flight blend 1,239/1,247: zero errors fixed, five introduced. Family macro F1 changed from .99543 to .99161. At +/-4 the net was -6 (one fixed, seven introduced). The incumbent heads had previously trained on these matches, so this is not a fair estimate of comparative transfer or a 99% product accuracy claim. No epoch or blend was selected using this result. Korea Masters 0058 is the predesignated separate development comparison, not an acceptance match. The four reserved acceptance videos remain unscored.

### Korea Masters development transfer (0058): do not promote

One external broadcast, 15 source-defined rallies, 155 published actions. Four frozen classifiers processed the same automatically tracked players/shuttle and 144 predicted within-rally contacts. 140 known actions bound uniquely under the frozen interval rule; 15 did not, and four predicted contacts remained unbound. Six-family macro F1 and all-family correct on those 140: ensemble .61088 / 83; window .63507 / 83; five-frame visual .63565 / 84; long-flight .63659 / 86. Flight fixed 5 and introduced 2 relative to window, or 4 and 2 relative to the visual head. The +0.15 percentage-point six-family gain vs window is not a meaningful demonstrated advance. Of 54 flight-head errors, 17 were source push predicted lift and 15 were source block predicted net. Both source guidelines and class support matter: only two labeled clears and five labeled lifts before matching. Do not merge or relabel these classes to improve the score. No whole-match or exact-time detection claim. All four final reservation matches remain unscored. Use only independently identified **training** broadcasts for taxonomy-aligned supervision; any future model informed by this diagnostic needs another untouched comparison. Full confusion and provenance: `.cache/stroke-transfer-dev-0058/transfer-score.json`.

### Training-only taxonomy audit: materially different targets on the same play

Compared FineBadminton 0033/0034/0035/0036 with ShuttleSet training matches 29/30/6/4 using aligned native ordinals, never predictions or family labels for matching. 116 rallies matched; 1,139 temporally unambiguous paired actions, of which 892 had eligible family labels on both sides. Only 600/892 agreed (67.3%); these are selected annotation pairs, not an error rate for either source. Of 155 ShuttleSet lifts, 106 were published as FineBadminton pushes and only 40 as lifts. Of 184 ShuttleSet net shots, 51 were FineBadminton blocks and 105 net shots. These two disagreements alone are 157/292 of the discrepancies and coincide with the directions of the two largest Korea errors. Do not treat a label translation, source-specific prior or label merge as an improved recognizer. Do not jointly train hard labels on these disputed pairs until a common taxonomy is checked. `.cache/stroke-source-audit/training-label-review-v1/reviewer/` holds 47 blinded training-video references, including agreement controls, on hash-verified videos; adjudication is outstanding. `training/audit_cross_corpus_stroke_labels.py` reproduces the paired audit. No final references/predictions were used.

### Is there a learnable distinction behind the source disagreements?

Training-only diagnostic, holding out one of the four overlapping original matches at a time. Given the first publisher's family and approximate frame, a fixed regularized logistic classifier uses automatic player boxes and image-plane shuttle positions/velocities before and after the hint to predict the second publisher's binary distinction. No threshold selection, final data, human adjudication or Korea predictions. On the 146 preselected lift/push pairs the classifier got 131 correct vs 106 for the fitting matches' majority label (26 fixed, 1 introduced); on 156 net/block pairs, 124 vs 105 (35 fixed, 16 introduced). Both comparisons favored the descriptor on every held-out match. Nine and 28 third-label examples respectively are outside this conditional task. The high lift/push score is not recognizer accuracy: it assumes a provided source family and approximate timing, and predicts a publisher, not the project's adjudicated truth. A deployable experiment must route from model predictions, retain other classes, and evaluate on new broadcasts. Six blinded training examples received a provisional visual pass, three remained unknown; do not promote those provisional judgments to hard ground truth.

### Hangzhou transfer: meaningful single-broadcast gain; candidate frozen

All 35 predefined rallies extracted, 349 source actions retained, 340 uniquely matched; nine ambiguous/unmatched. One-way lift-to-push specialist: 266/340 correct vs ensemble 242 and motion window 236. Six-family macro F1 .76088 vs .69072 and .67451. Lift/push macro F1 .74224 vs .38803 and .35610. Against ensemble 27 errors fixed and three introduced; against window 30 fixed and none introduced. Both frozen development thresholds passed. This is one publisher's action-interval benchmark, not independently adjudicated whole-match or side-specific accuracy. Keep incumbent application weights. Frozen components, score and source hashes in `.cache/stroke-transfer-dev-0043/candidate-freeze.json`; no further tuning on this broadcast. Next step is independent reference completion for the already reserved matches and frozen product-taxonomy comparison before any promotion decision.

### Frozen-router cross-check on the original ShuttleSet benchmark (no retuning)

`training/replay_stroke_route_historical.py` verified the Hangzhou-frozen router hash, reused the five complete original validation match packs, reproduced teacher-adjusted contacts and both historical score dictionaries exactly, and applied the frozen 0.75 lift-to-push rule. At +/-2 frames (2,036 matched known actions) historical matched-family macro F1: ensemble .80068, window .80275, routed .76480. Against window: 57 fixed, 156 introduced, net -99; per-match net -20, -25, -25, +8, -37. Complete-event F1 .84397 -> .82063. Window vs routed source-lift/predicted-push counts: 62 vs 218. At +/-4 frames, same -99 net of 2,062 matched known actions. This is a real regression under ShuttleSet's definitions, not a contradictory score for the same metric/population as Hangzhou. Combined with the dual-publisher training audit, this rules out treating the current router as a general-purpose upgrade. Keep it frozen for research, do not select a new threshold on either benchmark, and do not replace the installed classifier. The outstanding decision is the application label rubric, independently applied to original video; retain both label sources rather than overwriting either. Scoring artifacts: `.cache/lift-push-route-v1/historical-shuttleset.json`.
